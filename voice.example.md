# Your writing voice (optional)

Copy this file to `voice.md` (same folder) and edit it, or point
`DAYBOARD_VOICE_FILE` at it. When present, Dayboard's AI drafts replies in your
voice instead of a generic one. Delete `voice.md` (or leave it unset) to go back
to the default. Keep it short - a few lines plus one or two real examples works
better than a long essay.

## How I write
- Tone: warm but direct; no corporate filler.
- Greeting: first name only ("Hi Priya,").
- Length: 2-3 sentences, gets to the point fast.
- Sign-off: "Thanks, Pawni" (just "Pawni" for quick internal notes).
- Quirks: I say "happy to" not "I would be delighted to"; I avoid exclamation marks.

## A couple of real examples
> Hi Rahul, approved her access just now - she should be unblocked. Ping me if
> anything's still locked. Thanks, Pawni

> Hi Priya, sending the revised proposal by 1pm with the updated pricing. I'll
> flag the two line items that changed so the review is quick. Thanks, Pawni
